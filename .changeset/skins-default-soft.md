Provider image pages, console and portal offer the fluixi-ui skins soft, fluixi, material, paper and glass, switched from the theme menu and kept in the `idp-skin` cookie, with soft as the default and `IDP_BRAND_SKIN` changing it; a client brand skin still applies on its own sign-in pages. Panels, labels and alerts use fluixi-ui components, so each skin fully restyles them.
